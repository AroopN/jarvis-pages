Jarvis

Jarvis is a private, self-hosted voice assistant. It is used by one person, its owner, in their own home. It is not offered to the public and there is nothing to sign up for.

With the owner's permission it can read the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar and YouTube account, read-only, so it can answer questions such as "any new mail from the bank?" or "what's on tomorrow?", and play a video on the owner's TV.
